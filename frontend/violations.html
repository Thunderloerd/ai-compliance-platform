Violations

Track and manage compliance violations across your organization

What are violations?

Violations are records in your connected company database that break the compliance rules extracted from your policy PDFs. Each row here is one violating record: it shows the policy rule (clause), severity, status (pending / approved / dismissed), evidence (the actual data that failed the rule), and an AI-generated explanation of why it violates. Connect your database in Settings, upload policy PDFs to create rules, then run a scan from the Dashboard.
